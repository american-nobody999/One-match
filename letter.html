Good morning my dears,
I hope this letter finds you well and in the grace of the good spirits protecting you.

I can't quit you Palestine. Stay with me. We move forward together. I hear your voice. I hear your SOS.

I was nothing, no one without any purpose, any direction, gently floating in the distractions and the complicity of consumption and doing what I was told.

Thank you for saving me from that. Thank you for opening my eyes, and for showing me my humanity again. Thank you for showing me what real faith looks like.

Now, beyond the pretty words, its time for us to go to work. I am developing this website and this platform to meet our needs. We face major challenges, but communication is no longer one of those.

The contact form on the contact page is now the only way to officially communicate with me. Your name, email, and short message. You can include any ideas you have about the design and features of this website, after all, we are building this future together, to be added, or to be removed.

I am going to open up opportunities in the coming weeks as contests for financial compensation. Doc M and the other Palestrians gave me the idea. It's fair. You should be compensated for your content.

Palestine, I have not actually contacted or tried to communicate with your official government or civic leaders. I have most definitly annoyed other world leaders and governements

My priority is you the people. I have a few I watch, and I follow their social medias, but to be honest, until I can get a United States Ambassador and Embassy for Palestine, I feel I can't offer them any help. I'm also American, and my perspective is shaped by that. Lots of things to learn the hard about diplomacy and cultural differences.

Keep going. Steadfast. You remain. As long as there is breath left in my human body, Palestine will remain.

Be in touch Soon Loves,

Take Care of each other.

Leslie, American Nobody

والآن، بعيدًا عن الكلمات الجميلة، حان الوقت لنبدأ العمل. أنا أُطوّر هذا الموقع وهذه المنصة لتلبية احتياجاتنا. نحن نواجه تحديات كبيرة، لكن التواصل لم يعد واحدًا منها.
نموذج الاتصال في صفحة التواصل هو الآن الطريقة الرسمية الوحيدة للتواصل معي. الاسم، والبريد الإلكتروني، ورسالة قصيرة. يمكنكِ إضافة أي أفكار لديكِ حول تصميم هذا الموقع وميزاته، ففي النهاية نحن نبني هذا المستقبل معًا، ليُضاف أو يُزال منه ما نراه مناسبًا.
