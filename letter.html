Good Morning my loves,
I hope this letter finds you...
This week has passed and has brought many storms in many forms.
This week, from you, I learned that patience does not mean your ignored, it means trust that prayers were heard, this is a time that they are being answered in different ways we cannot see.
I also learned that peace is the most powerful weapon in the world, sometimes, being silence does not mean your not speaking, it means your protecting those you love from words and actions that cannot be undone.
I also learned that love is the strongest force in the universe, it can heal wounds, mend broken hearts, and bring light to the darkest places.
Every Single Palestinian matters, every single one. I understand the choice between your only means of communication and food or medicine for a sick child.
I am going to continue working and developing your gift, I just wanted you to know I am with you.
I do not know how, but we will find a way. I also want you to know just how much you mean to the world. People really no longer buy designer brands, they are wearing anything Palestine. You are everywhere. Watermelons. Palestine flags. You are the topic of most conversations. It seems like the world has abandoned you, and that is part of the daily life you have been forced to struggle through for years. It is not true.
You are the future peacekeepers and humantarians. I believe in you. Please take care of each other. With all my love,
L صباح الخير يا أحبائي،

أنتم صانعو السلام والإنسانيون في المستقبل. أنا أؤمن بكم. من فضلكم اعتنوا ببعضكم البعض.
