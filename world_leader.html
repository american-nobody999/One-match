An Appeal to the Leaders of the Worldنداء إلى قادة العالم

November 13, 2026١٣ نوفمبر ٢٠٢٦

Dear World Leaders,إلى قادة العالم الكرام،

We are writing with a simple request on behalf of our humanitarian mission for Palestine.نكتب إليكم بطلب بسيط في إطار مهمتنا الإنسانية من أجل فلسطين.

Please do not forget the people of Palestine.نرجوكم ألّا تنسوا شعب فلسطين.

Families need safety. Children need food, clean water, medicine, shelter, and the opportunity to live without fear.تحتاج العائلات إلى الأمان. ويحتاج الأطفال إلى الغذاء والمياه النظيفة والدواء والمأوى، وإلى فرصة للعيش بلا خوف.

Humanitarian crossings must remain open. Aid must reach the people who need it. Civilians must be protected, and international humanitarian law must be respected.يجب أن تبقى المعابر الإنسانية مفتوحة. ويجب أن تصل المساعدات إلى من يحتاجون إليها. ويجب حماية المدنيين واحترام القانون الدولي الإنساني.

We ask you to put aside political differences long enough to work together to protect human life.نطلب منكم تنحية الخلافات السياسية جانبًا بما يكفي للعمل معًا على حماية حياة البشر.

No child should have to wait for governments to agree before receiving food or medical care.لا ينبغي لأي طفل أن يضطر إلى انتظار اتفاق الحكومات قبل أن يحصل على الغذاء أو الرعاية الطبية.

No family should have to wonder whether the world has forgotten them.ولا ينبغي لأي عائلة أن تضطر إلى التساؤل عمّا إذا كان العالم قد نسيها.

We recognize every effort to bring relief, protect civilians, and pursue a lasting peace. We ask that those efforts continue.نقدّر كل جهد يُبذل لتقديم الإغاثة وحماية المدنيين والسعي إلى سلام دائم. ونطلب أن تستمر هذه الجهود.

You do not have to agree on everything to agree that human lives matter.ليس عليكم أن تتفقوا على كل شيء لكي تتفقوا على أن حياة البشر مهمة.

Please keep Palestine in your conversations, your decisions, and your actions.نرجوكم أن تُبقوا فلسطين حاضرة في أحاديثكم وقراراتكم وأفعالكم.

With respect and hope,مع الاحترام والأمل،

MPGA — American NobodyMPGA — أمريكان نوبودي

An Appreciation for Keeping Palestine in Focusتقدير لإبقاء فلسطين في دائرة الاهتمام

To the people of Iran, Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi, and Iran's diplomatic representatives around the world:

Thank you for continuing to bring attention to the suffering of Palestinians in Gaza, even while your own country faces uncertainty and serious challenges.

Your recent statements about the humanitarian crisis, the flooding of displaced families' shelters, and the lack of international attention have not gone unnoticed.

We agree that the crossings must be opened for humanitarian assistance. Civilians must be protected. The killings, suffering, and reported atrocities that the world has watched unfold must end, and allegations of violations must be independently investigated.

We cannot allow the people of Palestine to disappear from the world's attention whenever another international crisis takes over the headlines.

To Iran's embassies and diplomatic representatives: thank you for keeping Gaza in the conversation.

And on a lighter note, current world leaders sometimes provide more than enough material for our satire department.

The material practically writes itself. We just make the memes. 😂

But beneath the humor is something serious.

Our focus remains on the people of Palestine, humanitarian access, accountability, and the protection of civilian lives.

We can acknowledge those who speak about Palestinian suffering without agreeing on every other political issue.

Keep the attention on Palestine.
Keep the humanitarian crossings open.
Keep civilians at the center of the conversation.

With appreciation,
