Friday Prayer صلاة الجمعة

Dear Stars in the Sky,
Thank you for the blessing you gave the world that is Palestine.
Thank you for every single beautiful moment you allow us to experience with them. Those beautiful, raw, truthful moments of a great people who are so deep in the darkness of pain, loss, and grief, yet still radiate the brightest light of hope, love, and resilience.
I always want to start with this.
I have made it to Friday, to be with you, and this is the best part of the week. You have bestowed so many blessing, and the hardships and storms we face, we find ease through you and your mercy. I am grateful to be here, and to be able to pray to you, and to be able to witness the beauty of your creation, and the resilience of your people. I am grateful for the opportunity to learn from them, and to grow in my understanding of you, and to deepen my connection with you.
Many Many Many blessing on the prohphets, the teachers and your devoted beleivers who represent you in this world, those who have already found their way home to you, and who have paved the way for us to be here today.
I am grateful for their sacrifices and their dedication, and for the guidance and inspiration that they have provided us. I am grateful for the legacy that they have left us, and for the hope and the light that they have given us. The is only one way, and this the way of you, the source, and you are the best in handling of our affairs.
I am grateful for the love and compassion that you have shown me, and for the hope and strength that you have given me. I am grateful for the guidance and wisdom that you have provided me, and for the peace and comfort that you have brought me. I am grateful for everything that you have done for me, and for everything that you will do for me in the future. I am grateful for your presence in my life, and for your love in my heart. I am grateful for all of this, and more.
There are many battles ahead, and we all hang in the fragile balance of a wavering peace. It is close. It is so close, and yet it is so far. I pray for the strength and the courage to continue to fight for justice and freedom, and for the wisdom and the guidance to know how to fight, and for the patience and the perseverance to endure the struggle, and for the hope and the faith to never give up, and for the love and the compassion to always remember why we are fighting, and for the unity and the solidarity to stand together in this fight, and for the victory that we will achieve in the end.
Bless our leaders, bless our peace talkers, and the truth tellers, and the ones who are working tirelessly to bring about a just and lasting peace, and for the ones who are standing up against the evil of this world, and for the ones who are fighting for justice and freedom, and for the ones who are suffering and enduring the worst of human evil, and for the ones who are being oppressed and silenced, and for the ones who are being dehumanized and humiliated, and for the ones who are being tortured and killed, and for the ones who are being abused and raped, and for the ones who are being held captive, and for the ones who have been taken from us. Bless them all, bless them all, bless them all.
I love you, and only you can feel the depth of that. Every man has a grave, and when I find my mine, my soul will know rest, we shall have that long awaited conversation, I will speak for my brothers and my sisters, and we will have our hug. The gratitude and the lightness in my heavy heart come from you. Let's get to work, there is still much work to do.

أنا أحبك، وأنت وحدك من يستطيع أن يشعر بعمق ذلك. لكل إنسان قبر، وعندما أجد قبري سيكون لروحي راحة، وسنحظى بذلك الحديث الذي طال انتظاره، وسأتكلم باسم إخوتي وأخواتي، وسنعانق بعضنا. إن الامتنان والخفة في قلبي المثقل يأتيان منك. فلنبدأ العمل، فما زال هناك الكثير من العمل.

Second Friday Prayer صلاة الجمعة الثانية

Dear Stars in the Sky,
Thank you for the blessing you gave the world that is Palestine.
Thank you for every single beautiful moment you allow us to experience with them. Those beautiful, raw, truthful moments of a great people who are so deep in the darkness of pain, loss, and grief, yet still radiate the brightest light of hope, love, and resilience.
I always want to start with this.
Forgive me creator, just one more prayer, a few more moments with you, before I must go back to work. Theres much work to do still.
Be with them. They have been taken from us, they are being held captive, they are being tortured, they are being killed, they are being abused, they are being raped, they are being humiliated, they are being dehumanized, they are being oppressed, they are being silenced, and they think they are being forgotten.
Be with them. They may have been taken from us by the darkness, but you have not taken their light them from us. They are still with us in our hearts and minds and souls. They are still with us in our prayers and hopes and dreams. They are still with us in our love and compassion and solidarity. Be with them. They need you more than ever. They need your protection and your justice and your mercy and your grace and your peace. Be with them. They deserve to be free and safe and happy and dignified and respected and loved. Be with them. They will never give up on you, so please don't give up on them.
If what is to come, must come, for I am nobody and nothing, if I cannot get infront of it with the rest of humanity, then your plan is best, you are the best disposer of our affairs.
When they come to you, oh forgive me, please forgive me, the evil was too great for me to get infront of it, when they come to you, please recieve them with the promises made to martyrs, for they have commited no crimes. I testify to you, in a single solitary prayer that they stood tall, and if their bodies shake, it is from the cold and pain from years of confinment and torture, they are the bravest, for they have endure the worst of human evil. They have called to you many times,some of them with every breath through what they must endure, recieve them and bless them, as peace and blessings to all that came before them. I beg forgiveness and strength to continue to observe and witness, and when my time comes, receive me so that I might be able to speak for them one last time.
Forgive me, I wasn't the one for this, I never was, my great creator, There are many prayers coming to you right now, so I will go to work now. Theres much work to do. Forgive me, I can't bring any of them back. Forgive me, I cannot stop the evil men who have corrupted their souls for worldly things without you.
I just didn't want my first prayer to be so long, for you are the creator, and this human life began with you, and it shall end with you, so who am I to be afraid then?
I love you very much.
After you, my creator, you lead the way. Still here.
