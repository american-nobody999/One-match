Dear Stars in the Sky,
Thank you for the blessing you gave the world that is Palestine.

Oh my beloved one, I am at a lost for words. I don't know what to say to them anymore. My voice is yours. but I don't know what to say. Have you not heard this before? I don't know what to say to make it better for anyone. oh my love, I have no idea what we are doing, all I know is this your plan, your will, and my steadfastness to remain in your service for it.
I want to come home to you, when the time comes, when this life is over, and all the temporary false rewards fall away at the graves, I want that chance to speak for my beloved believers, not to collect a reward. I want to tell you about the ones who proved to you their faith was true, despite the hardships and struggles that they were tested with. I will sit with you in infitinity and sing songs and tell stories about how the great people, how truly great they were, because they chose you, and the right ways, your ways.

I will speak of those who refused to bow to the evil or who refused to be apart of it. May it please you. May it bring you joy.

The flaws are between us, great creator, and we will discuss those failures in the end as well. Please forgive the small things, and the big things, and the things I can't even see I'm doing.
My creator, I don't want to leave this space. I never do. Week after week, day after day, I never want to go from you, back out into the world, even though you refill me, make me whole, heal my broken peices, and go with me, when I am laid before you, this is the safest place I can find.
My great one, protect our people. Protect our true leaders. Protect our brothers and sisters, may they find ease in thousand little ways this week, and if it is your will to bring them home, may the hurting and pained hearts know some comfort in that they are back home with you.
Lets go to work, my anchor to these high waves, my wind to these sails, my word when I can find none. May this path lead home to you, and to the freedom, truth and justice due to the people. Be with us as all our billions of little boats all sail towards the people, towards the truth.
Also, please help me stop using curse words against the occupation and their friends, even though they deserve them. This is not funny, this is serious. It is a flaw that I'm struggling really hard with. Controlling this spicy American mouth is most definitly something I'm going to work on for you this week. If I make it a promise to you, then it has to happen, any way we can.
I love you so much. I love our people so much. Lets go get it.
