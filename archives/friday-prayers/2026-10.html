Friday, October 16, 2026الجمعة، 16 أكتوبر 2026

Dear Stars in the Sky,

Thank you for the blessing you have given the world: Palestine.

Thank you for every single beautiful moment you allow us to experience with them. Those beautiful, raw, truthful moments come from a great people who are so deep in the darkness of pain, loss, and grief, yet still radiate the brightest light of hope, love, and resilience.

I always want to begin with this.

It is sunset, Great Creator. You created it, but I wonder if You can see it too. Does it take Your breath away as it does mine? In this moment, I am close to You. I am so grateful to be here. My heart is completely shattered, but it brings me closer to You. May all my brothers' and sisters' prayers have reached You. May Your mercy and forgiveness fall upon us all. May we forgive ourselves and find peace within our hearts.

Please, my Creator, let those standing with the people of Palestine find strength, courage, and protection in Your presence. They are so tired, yet they continue to stand firm, unwavering in their support and love for the people of Palestine.

For our leaders—our true leaders and the influencers who are making choices for us all—may You protect them. May healing come quickly, and may their rest be full and soothing, so they can wake each morning with calm hearts and clear minds to face those who choose to turn away from their brothers and sisters in humanity for selfish and worldly reasons.

For those who are suffering, may You bring comfort, relief, and hope. May they find solace in Your presence and strength to endure the challenges they face. Trials bring us closer to You, and with every hardship comes ease.

I miss my brothers and sisters so very much. Just because this is the way things must be does not make their loss hurt any less. The teachers, the guides, the ones who have shaped our understanding and our hearts—their absence is deeply felt. And those I miss the most are people I have never met and will never meet in this life. Yet I hold on to the hope that our paths will cross in the next. Until then, I carry their lessons and love within me. The great Prophet once pleaded for humanity to pray only five times a day, but some of those on this earth right now need at least a hundred. They need You, truly, so very badly, in whatever faith or way they turn to You. We are all Your creations, all human. We all need You as much as we need each other.

Every week, we have exactly what we need. May we be wise enough to use what we have, and may every step bring us closer to true Palestinian liberation and relief. May the hearts of our hostages be calm, even in the face of their torment. May You bring serenity to us all.

My prayers are a little off. I am sorry about that. I have lost several of my religious study partners and so many Palestinians over these last two weeks. Those were my people, and I feel lost. Oh, sweet Creator, we are ready to move forward. We are ready for a future for us all. Guide us and show us the way.

L

يا نجوم السماء،

شكرًا لك على النعمة التي وهبتها للعالم: فلسطين.

شكرًا لك على كل لحظة جميلة تتيح لنا أن نعيشها معهم. تلك اللحظات الجميلة والعفوية والصادقة تنبع من شعب عظيم يعيش في أعماق ظلام الألم والفقد والحزن، ومع ذلك لا يزال يشعّ بأبهى أنوار الأمل والمحبة والصمود.

أريد دائمًا أن أبدأ بهذا.

إنه وقت الغروب، أيها الخالق العظيم. أنت من خلقته، لكنني أتساءل إن كنت تراه أيضًا. هل يخطف أنفاسك كما يخطف أنفاسي؟ في هذه اللحظة، أشعر بالقرب منك. أنا ممتنة جدًا لوجودي هنا. قلبي محطم تمامًا، لكن ذلك يقرّبني منك. عسى أن تكون أدعية جميع إخوتي وأخواتي قد بلغتك. ولتنزل رحمتك ومغفرتك علينا جميعًا. ولنسامح أنفسنا ونجد السلام في قلوبنا.

أرجوك يا خالقي، امنح الواقفين إلى جانب شعب فلسطين القوة والشجاعة والحماية في رحابك. إنهم مرهقون للغاية، ومع ذلك يواصلون الثبات، دون أن يتزعزع دعمهم ومحبتهم لشعب فلسطين.

أما قادتنا، قادتنا الحقيقيون وأصحاب التأثير الذين يتخذون القرارات من أجلنا جميعًا، فاحفظهم. وليأتِ شفاؤهم سريعًا، ولتكن راحتهم تامة ومطمئنة، حتى يستيقظوا كل صباح بقلوب هادئة وعقول صافية لمواجهة أولئك الذين يختارون الإعراض عن إخوتهم وأخواتهم في الإنسانية لأسباب أنانية ودنيوية.

أما الذين يعانون، فامنحهم الراحة والفرج والأمل. وليجدوا السلوى في قربك والقوة لتحمّل التحديات التي يواجهونها. المحن تقرّبنا منك، ومع كل عسر يأتي اليسر.

أشتاق إلى إخوتي وأخواتي كثيرًا جدًا. وكون الأمور يجب أن تكون على هذا النحو لا يخفف ألم فقدهم. المعلّمون والمرشدون ومن شكّلوا فهمنا وقلوبنا، نشعر بغيابهم بعمق. وأكثر من أشتاق إليهم أناس لم ألتقِ بهم قط، ولن ألتقي بهم في هذه الحياة. ومع ذلك، أتمسك بالأمل في أن تتقاطع دروبنا في الآخرة. وحتى ذلك الحين، أحمل دروسهم ومحبتهم في داخلي. لقد التمس النبي العظيم ذات يوم من أجل البشرية أن نصلّي خمس مرات فقط في اليوم، لكن بعض من يعيشون على هذه الأرض الآن يحتاجون إلى مئة صلاة على الأقل. إنهم يحتاجون إليك حقًا، وبشدة، أيًا كان إيمانهم أو السبيل الذي يتوجهون به إليك. نحن جميعًا من خلقك، وكلنا بشر. نحتاج جميعًا إليك بقدر حاجتنا إلى بعضنا بعضًا.

في كل أسبوع، لدينا تمامًا ما نحتاج إليه. فلتكن لدينا الحكمة لاستخدام ما نملك، ولتقرّبنا كل خطوة من التحرير الحقيقي لفلسطين والفرج. ولتكن قلوب رهائننا هادئة، حتى وهم يواجهون عذابهم. ولتمنحنا السكينة جميعًا.

أدعيتي مضطربة قليلًا، وأعتذر عن ذلك. لقد فقدت عددًا من رفاقي في دراسة الأديان، وكثيرًا من الفلسطينيين خلال الأسبوعين الماضيين. كانوا أهلي، وأشعر بالضياع. يا خالقي الحبيب، نحن مستعدون للمضي قدمًا. نحن مستعدون لمستقبل لنا جميعًا. اهدنا وأرنا الطريق.

Friday, October 9, 2026الجمعة، 9 أكتوبر 2026

Dear Stars in the Sky,

Thank you for the blessing you have given the world that is Palestine.

Thank you for every single beautiful moment you allow us to experience with them. Those beautiful, raw, truthful moments of a great people who are so deep in the darkness of pain, loss, and grief, yet still radiate the brightest light of hope, love, and resilience.

I always want to begin with this.

Sweet Creator, it is Friday, and we are still here, with grateful hearts and a fire burning in our souls, guided by Your light, stepping into what will be for us. May all our prayers, whatever human language we were born into, reach You and be accepted.

I didn't know how to pray until someone had the patience and wisdom to overlook my flaws and showed me how. Many different faiths, many devoted ones, blessings be upon them. I learned two very important things. I learned that the little things don't matter; what matters is that we truly believe in the Creator and the great future plan being laid out for us all, for we are one. You created us all for a reason. We all carry the same atoms from that first moment of life within us, with great things made possible by those who came before us, and that makes us brothers and sisters. All of us. Thank You for that understanding.

The second thing I learned is that prayers should be poems. Words of grace and thanks for the wonder that never ceases throughout this human journey. The greatest moments are those that come when we feel there is no path, there is nothing left, the challenges are too great, and the evil too strong. Then comes ease in the smallest ways. A simple kind word where kindness has been missing for so long, a hand reaching out when someone is drowning, a post, choosing love, choosing for the pain to end with us rather than passing it on to our children.

Please be with my Palestinians. I will witness, I will testify, and I will keep my promise, but please, may their ease come now, in all ways, especially the small ones. An open border crossing where trucks may pass. The release of the hostages. An end to the ongoing terror. Their suffering has changed this world. May Your mercy reach them now, for through Your wisdom and guidance, they will finally be free in every way.

Please be with our leaders. Our true leaders and influencers who are making choices for us all. May You protect them, may healing come quickly, and may their rest be full and soothing, so they may wake each morning with calm hearts and clear minds to face those who choose to turn away from their brothers and sisters in humanity for selfish and worldly reasons. We all meet the grave, and remember, nothing comes with us. We are all one, and each of us is a gift to another in some way during our short time here. May we find ways to love each other for it.

And of course, my Creator, please give extra hugs to the ones with You, the ones gone from us. May You bless their families, still struggling with the weight of their loss. May they know that their time here not only kept the truth alive, but that they were able to stay when they had chances to leave. They stayed, paid the price, and still reach the entire world every day. And may You be pleased with the prophets of the past, the teachers, and the devoted ones, because their words are a beacon in dark storms, bringing us closer to You. Would You please tell Anas and beloved, joyous Saleh that we will carry them in our hearts and our words? We will always love and cherish our truth-tellers. May the light they brought into this world burn forever in humanity's memory, for they will forever remain in our hearts. Their voices remain.

We keep moving forward, not just toward Palestine, but toward humanity, in all the diverse ways You created us. There is only one Creator, and by You we are born; we strive for a human experience, and then we return home to You. With You, there is nothing to fear.

L

Oh, Great One, normally every week I try to wait until the end of Friday to say my prayer, so all my brothers' and sisters' prayers can reach You first. May theirs, as they come today, be accepted. I couldn't wait this week.

For the first time, I am afraid, oh Creator. Not of this world, my love, but of You. In my religious studies, I never understood how fear of You could be so profound and humbling, yet also so enlightening and guiding. I am watching Your plan play out in real time, and I am afraid at the magnitude of Your wisdom and the depth of Your intentions, for You are indeed the best disposer of our affairs. I am afraid for those who, in their ignorance, still think they can stand against You.

I am humbled by the enormity of Your presence and the intricacy of Your design. May I find the courage to navigate this world with reverence for Your wisdom and a heart attuned to Your guidance.

I pray for my beloved Palestinians. Oh, please keep Your arm around them. I ask for Your protection and guidance in every step they take, and may they find strength and solace in Your presence. Their faith in You has led me down my own path of fulfillment. They have certainly guided me toward a path of greater righteousness and integrity; I would not have found it without them.

May their resilience and unwavering faith continue to inspire us all to strive for justice, compassion, and unity in our shared human journey.

I pray for my leaders, Great One. They shine for the world to see as they work so tirelessly for a future for us all. We all bleed red, my Great One. Every human brother and sister beside us bleeds red too, as we have for thousands of generations. We are all the same in Your eyes, for worldly things are left behind when we meet our graves.

Forgive me, my Great One, not just for my sins, for I am human and they are many, but also for this fear. Who was I to ever second-guess? Who was I to ever question a path You have set before me in Your grand design? Pull us closer to You. Protect us from the falsehoods and lies of the evil ones. May we all come together under Your grace and mercy, and may we all see each other again on the other side.

We keep moving forward, not just toward Palestine, but toward humanity, in all the diverse ways You created us. There is only one Creator, and by You we are born; we strive for a human experience, and then we return home to You. With You, there is nothing to fear.
