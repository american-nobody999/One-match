Leslie's Quests مهام ليزلي

Quest 1

Golden Ticket to Palestine is your direct entry point to practical action. Use this page to access verified updates, shareable resources, and organizing tools that can be posted across your own networks without delay. The goal is to convert attention into concrete support by giving people one reliable place to start, learn, and move together. If someone asks what they can do right now, this is the first link to send. Bookmark this page so you can return to it quickly during fast-moving updates. Share it with at least one new person each day to widen participation. Treat it like a living action board, not a one-time post.

Quest 2

The Red Ribbons campaign advocates for the immediate release of more than 9,000 Palestinian detainees, including individuals held under administrative detention without charge or trial. The campaign also calls on the International Committee of the Red Cross (ICRC) and other international organizations to secure unrestricted access to detention facilities, inspect conditions, and check on detainees, including Dr. Hussam Abu Safiya. Organizers emphasize the experiences of vulnerable groups, particularly women, children, journalists, and medical professionals. Overall, the campaign seeks greater transparency, humanitarian oversight, and accountability regarding Palestinian detainees and urges international organizations to take action to protect their rights and well-being. Keep circulating verified detainee information so cases are not erased by news cycles. Contact organizations politely but persistently and ask for public updates on access and welfare checks. Archive statements and timelines so accountability work has a clear public record.

Quest 3

We need to promote civic groups and Palestinian humanitarian organizations on the ground. They dig the remains of our loved ones by hand while the occupation hides 50 million tons of rubble to cover war crimes. Highlight groups publishing transparent needs and field updates. Prioritize medics, rescue teams, and community kitchens, and keep repeating their names so they stay protected.

Quest 4

Families need immediate water access, consistent food distribution, emergency medicine, and basic education support that can keep children connected to learning under siege conditions. They also need sustained international pressure to stop attacks on civilians and civilian infrastructure. Support organizations that are transparent about delivery routes, field partners, and reporting so aid is not only announced but actually received. Every verified donation, post, and direct outreach message helps close the gap between promises and real survival on the ground. Ask providers for frequency data so communities know when help is expected to arrive. Share practical guidance on water safety, storage, and sanitation whenever trusted sources publish it. Push for long-term infrastructure repair, not only emergency drops.

Quest 5

Bisan and Gaza need continuous visibility because attacks keep targeting daily life, including shelters, hospitals, and public gatherings where people should be safest. Elevate eyewitness reporting, amplify trusted Palestinian journalists, and keep documenting events in real time so evidence does not disappear under misinformation cycles. This quest is about refusing silence: keep attention on patterns of harm, keep pressure on institutions, and keep humanitarian channels visible. Stay alert, stay coordinated, and keep your people informed with verified sources. Save source links with dates so important updates are easy to re-share accurately. Correct misinformation quickly and calmly with evidence-based references. Keep international audiences engaged by pairing facts with clear calls to action.

Quest 6

The 8th Front is the newest battlefield: a digital war over minds and souls waged across every platform, comment section, and algorithm. Photographs, testimony, and truth compete daily against misinformation, suppression, and disappearing content, and every share, screenshot, and saved file is an act of resistance. Learn how to preserve what matters, protect the voices at risk of being erased, and understand the platforms shaping what the world is allowed to see. This is not a battle fought with weapons; it is fought with attention, memory, and the refusal to look away. Visit the 8th Front to see the tools, tactics, and truths keeping this fight alive.

Quest 7

Board Of Peace is a civic accountability focus area that needs broad public scrutiny from Americans across every state and political background. Review the record, read the public claims, and compare outcomes against international law, humanitarian standards, and constitutional limits on executive authority. This quest is about visibility and pressure: the more people who evaluate, archive, and discuss the facts, the harder it becomes to normalize harmful policy choices. Invite lawyers, educators, and civic groups to publish independent analyses of these policies. Compare promises against measurable outcomes and keep that comparison public. When facts are organized clearly, public pressure becomes harder to ignore.
