Saleh Aljafarawi was the first Palestinian journalist I encountered in my research. Killed October 12, 2025, in Gaza City clashes just after the ceasefire—shot while documenting destruction in a press vest. His death became my catalyst. This story is a tribute to him: speaking what silence stole, a search and find of words when we don't know what to say anymore. كان صالح الجعفراوي أول صحفي فلسطيني صادفته في أبحاثي. قُتل في 12 أكتوبر 2025 في اشتباكات مدينة غزة بعد وقف إطلاق النار بقليل—أُطلق عليه الرصاص بينما كان يوثق الدمار مرتديًا سترة الصحافة. صار موته الشرارة التي دفعتني. هذه القصة تكريم له: قول ما سلبه الصمت، والبحث عن كلمات حين لا نعرف ماذا نقول بعد الآن.

Part 2: We Exist نحن موجودون ولن نُمحى.

Bisan Owda was the second. Bisan Owda is a young Palestinian journalist, activist, and filmmaker from Gaza. She rose to international prominence through her powerful social media videos and series documenting daily life, displacement, and survival amid the Gaza war
Her Instagram/TikTok content, often raw and unfiltered from shelters or amid destruction, has amassed millions of followers and brought global attention to the humanitarian crisis in Gaza. She produces the series It's Bisan from Gaza and I'm Still Alive for Al Jazeera's AJ+, which earned her a prestigious Peabody Award in the News category in 2024, along with an Edward R. Murrow Award recognition for her courageous frontline journalism.
Despite facing repeated displacement and danger, she continues to report as a voice from inside Gaza.
